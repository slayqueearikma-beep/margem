# MarGem Business Subscription Terms

**Last updated:** August 2026

These terms apply to MarGem business subscription plans for sellers and storefronts.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Billing |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| **Basic** | Free | Free | Included with every business account |
| **Premium** | 199 MAD | 1,999 MAD | Stripe Checkout / Customer Portal |
| **Enterprise** | 499 MAD | 3,999 MAD | Stripe Checkout / Customer Portal |

All prices are in Moroccan Dirham (MAD).

## Basic (Free)

- Assigned automatically when you create a business on MarGem
- No payment or Stripe checkout required
- Default plan when a paid subscription expires or is cancelled
- Includes a storefront, listings, messaging, and standard search visibility

## Premium and Enterprise

- Purchased through Stripe Checkout or changed via the Stripe Customer Portal
- Billed monthly or yearly at the prices shown above
- May include a free trial when enabled on the platform
- Provide enhanced visibility, badges, analytics, and support features as described in the app

## Cancellation

- You may cancel a paid plan at any time through the billing portal
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- After cancellation, your business reverts to the free Basic plan

## Refunds

Contact **billing@margem.ma** for billing questions. Refund handling follows our refund policy and applicable law.
