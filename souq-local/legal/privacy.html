MarGem Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MarGem connects buyers with local businesses in Morocco. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information (email, display name, account type)
  • Business information for sellers (name, address, phone, images, location)
  • Usage and crash diagnostics when enabled
  • Location only when you grant permission for map features

How we use information

  • Operate your account and listings
  • Enable messaging between buyers and businesses
  • Process subscriptions via Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Improve reliability and security

Your rights

You can delete your account from Settings → Account security in the app. You may export your data via the in-app export feature or contact privacy@margem.app.
