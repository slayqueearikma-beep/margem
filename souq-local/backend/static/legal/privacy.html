MarGem Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

MarGem collects account data (email, display name), seller profile information, listings, messages, and usage analytics needed to operate the marketplace.

Data we store

  • Account credentials (password hashes, never plaintext passwords)
  • Email verification and security logs
  • Product images and seller media uploaded by users
  • Messages, reviews, and community posts you create

Data we do not store

  • Full payment card numbers (handled by Stripe)
  • JWT or refresh tokens in server logs

Your rights

You may delete your account in the app. Data export is available from account settings where enabled. Contact privacy@margem.ma for privacy requests.

This policy is a technical placeholder and must be reviewed by legal counsel before commercial launch.
