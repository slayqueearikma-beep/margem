Account Deletion & Data Rights

Dribex · Version 2.0.0 · Effective 2026-08-13 · Last updated 2026-08-13

Français · العربية

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone after processing completes.

1. Your data rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion of your personal information. This document explains how to exercise those rights on Dribex.

2. Export your data

Signed-in users can export a copy of account information in Settings → Your data → Export. You may also request an export by emailing privacy@dribex.app from your registered address. We may verify your identity before responding.

3. Correct your data

Update profile fields in the app where available. For other corrections, contact privacy@dribex.app with details.

4. Delete your account

You may delete your account at any time:

  1. In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete account → confirm with your password
  2. By email: Send a deletion request from your registered email to privacy@dribex.app

Guest favorites and local preferences stored only on your device are removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

5. What we delete or anonymize

After confirmed deletion, we generally remove or anonymize:

  • Storefronts, products, services, and seller categories
  • Buyer–seller messages and conversations
  • Reviews you authored; reviews on your business (if you were a seller)
  • Favorites, follows, saved searches, notifications, and subscriptions
  • Community memberships; your community messages are anonymized
  • Authentication tokens and multi-factor authentication settings

6. What we may retain

We may retain limited information where required or permitted by law, including:

  • An anonymized account record to prevent re-registration abuse
  • Billing, tax, or invoicing records (typically up to seven years where required)
  • Security, fraud-prevention, and administrative audit logs
  • Backup copies for a limited period (typically up to ninety days)
  • Data subject to legal hold or ongoing disputes

7. Processing time

We aim to complete deletion within a reasonable period after verification. Some systems may take additional time to purge backups.

8. Before you delete

Export any information you wish to keep. Deletion does not automatically cancel off-platform obligations with other users or third parties.
