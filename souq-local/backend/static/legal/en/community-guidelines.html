Community Guidelines

Dribex · Version 2.0.0 · Effective 2026-08-13 · Last updated 2026-08-13

Français · العربية

Purpose: These guidelines help keep Dribex respectful, safe, and useful for buyers, sellers, and neighbors. They apply to reviews, messages, city communities, marketplace communities, and any future social or communication features.

1. Be honest

  • Provide accurate business information, prices, and availability
  • Write reviews based on genuine experiences
  • Do not impersonate people, businesses, or Dribex staff
  • Label promotional or sponsored content where required

2. Be respectful

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse
  • Disagree without attacking people or protected groups
  • Respect privacy — do not share others' personal data without permission

3. Be safe

  • Do not promote scams, fraud, illegal goods, or dangerous activities
  • Report suspicious listings, messages, or accounts using in-app tools or safety@dribex.ma
  • Take sensible precautions for offline meetings and payments

4. Marketplace integrity

  • No fake listings, bait-and-switch pricing, or review manipulation
  • No spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or abusive automation
  • Follow channel-specific rules in city and marketplace communities

5. Prohibited content

Do not post content that is illegal under Moroccan law or our policies, including counterfeit goods, stolen items, unlicensed medical claims, or content that exploits minors. You are responsible for the legality of what you post. Display or discovery of content on Dribex does not mean we approve or endorse it.

6. Moderation

We may remove content, limit visibility, suspend or ban accounts, or cooperate with competent authorities where legally required, using automated systems and human review. Enforcement decisions may not always include detailed explanations where safety or legal obligations require confidentiality.

7. Appeals

If you believe we made an error, contact support@dribex.ma with relevant details. We will review in good faith but cannot guarantee reversal.

8. Updates

We may update these guidelines as features evolve. Continued use of community features after notice constitutes acceptance to the extent permitted by law.
