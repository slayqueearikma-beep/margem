4. Why we use information

We process personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the platform and its current and future features
  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Enable discovery, listings, messaging, communities, notifications, and premium services
  • Verify identities, businesses, or eligibility where we offer verification features
  • Send service, security, and (where permitted) marketing communications
  • Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our terms
  • Analyze aggregated or pseudonymized usage to improve reliability and user experience

Our legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests, consent (where required), and legal obligation, depending on the activity and applicable law.

5. Communications and preferences

We may send transactional messages (verification, security alerts, service updates) necessary to operate your account. Optional marketing or personalized recommendations are used only according to your settings and applicable law. You can adjust certain preferences in the app and unsubscribe from promotional email where offered.

6. Sharing with service providers

We use trusted providers to host infrastructure, deliver email, store media, provide maps, monitor errors, and support security. They process data only on our instructions and for the purposes described in this policy. We may also share information:

  • With other users, as you choose (for example public listings or messages)
  • With authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer subject to appropriate safeguards

Current and future payment processors, identity verification vendors, analytics tools, and moderation systems will be described in updates to this policy or related notices before material new processing begins, where required by law.

7. International transfers

We are based in Kingdom of Morocco. Data may be processed in Morocco and in other countries where our providers operate. When transfers require safeguards, we implement appropriate contractual or organizational measures.

8. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by data type. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data as described in our Account Deletion & Data Rights document, except where law requires longer retention (for example tax, billing, or legal holds).

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Morocco Law 09-08 and, where relevant, GDPR or similar frameworks), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your data, and to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

You can:

  • Export account data in the app (Settings → Your data)
  • Request correction by contacting privacy@dribex.app
  • Delete your account in the app or by verified email request

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling requests.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures such as access controls, encryption in transit, secure credential storage, monitoring, and rate limiting. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Report security concerns to security@dribex.ma.

11. Children

Dribex is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations, or data practices change materially. We will update the effective date and, where required, notify you in the app or by email and request renewed consent if necessary. Continued use after notice does not, by itself, replace any consent or other legal requirement that applies to specific processing.
