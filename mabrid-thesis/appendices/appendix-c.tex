\chapter{Glossary of Marketplace Governance Terms}
\label{app:glossary-extended}

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=3cm]
  \item[Liquidity] Sufficient buyer and seller activity such that users reliably find counterparties and value on the platform.
  \item[Moderation] Human and assisted review of user-generated content against published community standards.
  \item[Verification] Process by which a seller demonstrates identity or business legitimacy to receive a trust badge.
  \item[Multi-sided platform] Economic model connecting two or more distinct user groups whose value increases with participation on both sides.
  \item[FinOps] Discipline combining finance, technology, and business to manage cloud unit economics.
  \item[Landing zone] Pre-configured cloud environment enforcing security, networking, and governance baselines.
  \item[Data controller] Entity determining purposes and means of personal data processing under privacy law.
  \item[Data processor] Entity processing personal data on behalf of the controller.
\end{description}
