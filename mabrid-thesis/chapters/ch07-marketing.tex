\chapter{Marketing Strategy}
\label{ch:marketing}

\section{Introduction}

Even the most trustworthy marketplace fails without awareness, adoption, and retention. This chapter defines MABRID's marketing strategy: brand positioning, customer acquisition, digital channels, community-led growth, and loyalty programmes---aligned with the trust-centric business vision and without reliance on deceptive growth tactics.

\section{Branding}

\subsection{Brand Identity}

MABRID brand expresses \emph{trusted local discovery}: approachable, professional, Moroccan-rooted, and modern. Visual identity (to be supplied as figures) uses consistent colour, typography, and logo lockups across touchpoints.

\subsection{Brand Promise}

``Find local businesses you can trust.'' The promise is operationalised through verification, moderation, and transparent policies---marketing must not overclaim beyond platform capabilities.

\subsection{Brand Architecture}

Master brand MABRID with sub-brands for city communities (e.g., MABRID Casablanca) and premium tiers (MABRID Pro for sellers).

\section{Positioning}

\subsection{Positioning Statement}

For Moroccan consumers and SMEs who struggle with fragmented local discovery, MABRID is the trusted marketplace that connects verified businesses with buyers through city-relevant search, reviews, and community---unlike generic classifieds or informal social selling because MABRID invests in governance, security, and compliance.

\section{Customer Acquisition}

\subsection{Buyer Acquisition}

Channels include search engine optimisation for local queries, app store optimisation, influencer partnerships with lifestyle and food creators, and PR around trust initiatives (verification milestones, city launches).

\subsection{Seller Acquisition}

Field outreach to business associations, chamber of commerce events, digital skills workshops, and referral incentives for accountants and web agencies serving SMEs.

\subsection{Acquisition Funnel}

Awareness $\rightarrow$ consideration (landing pages, case studies) $\rightarrow$ registration $\rightarrow$ activation (first listing or first search) $\rightarrow$ retention. Funnel metrics are reviewed weekly during growth phases.

\section{Digital Marketing}

\subsection{Search and Content}

Localised content marketing (blog, guides: ``Best plumbers in Rabat'') drives organic traffic while providing genuine user value. Content complies with advertising and consumer protection standards.

\subsection{Paid Media}

Paid social and search campaigns target city-level keywords with capped budgets and creative testing. Landing pages mirror ad promises to avoid bounce and regulatory complaints.

\subsection{Email and Lifecycle}

Transactional email (verification, alerts) is distinguished from marketing email (opt-in only). Lifecycle campaigns re-engage dormant buyers and educate new sellers on verification benefits.

\section{Social Media Strategy}

Platforms prioritised: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn (B2B sellers), and WhatsApp Business for seller support (not unsolicited buyer spam). Community managers respond in Arabic and French. Crisis communication playbooks integrate with trust \& safety.

\section{Referral Programme}

Dual-sided referral incentives reward buyers and sellers who invite verified participants. Anti-fraud controls prevent synthetic account farming. Referral terms are published to avoid pyramid scheme perceptions.

\section{Customer Retention}

\subsection{Buyer Retention}

Saved searches, favourites, personalised recommendations (with privacy controls), city community engagement, and push notifications for followed businesses increase return visits.

\subsection{Seller Retention}

Success metrics dashboards, responsive support, premium ROI case studies, and verification badge prestige reduce churn. Exit interviews inform product roadmap.

\subsection{Loyalty Principles}

Loyalty is earned through reliability, not dark patterns. Unsubscribe and account deletion remain accessible per privacy policy.

\section{Growth Strategy Integration}

Marketing aligns with Chapter~\ref{ch:roadmap} phases: heavy local investment in anchor cities before national media spend. Marketing budget as percentage of revenue tightens as organic share grows.

\section{Chapter Conclusion}

MABRID's marketing strategy reinforces---never undermines---its trust positioning. Disciplined branding, localised acquisition, ethical digital campaigns, and retention-focused product marketing together build a sustainable user base capable of supporting national expansion and long-term financial sustainability.
