> **Note:** This policy describes cookies and similar technologies on our **website**. Mobile apps use secure local storage and preferences instead of browser cookies.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. We use them to operate the site, remember preferences, and (where permitted) measure performance.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration | Category |
|-------------|---------|----------|----------|
| Session / authentication | Keep you signed in on the web | Session | Strictly necessary |
| Language preference | Remember your language choice | Up to 12 months | Functional |
| Cookie consent | Store your cookie choices when a banner is shown | Up to 12 months | Functional |
| Security | Help detect abuse and protect accounts | Session or short-term | Strictly necessary |
| Analytics (if enabled) | Understand usage and improve the service | As configured | Optional |

If we enable third-party maps or embedded content, those providers may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

## 3. Mobile app storage

Our mobile apps do not use browser cookies. Instead, they may store:

- Encrypted authentication tokens (secure storage)
- Language, onboarding, and privacy preference settings
- Guest favorites and cached content for performance

You can clear app storage through your device settings or delete your account to remove account-linked data from our systems.

## 4. Your choices

- **Browser:** Use browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may affect sign-in and core features.
- **Consent banner:** Where required, we will ask before non-essential cookies. You can change choices through the banner or site settings when available.
- **App:** Manage optional preferences in **Settings → Privacy** where offered.

## 5. Changes

We will update this policy when our cookies or providers change materially. See our Privacy Policy for broader data practices.
