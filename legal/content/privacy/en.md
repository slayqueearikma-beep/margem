## 4. Why we use information

We process personal information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the platform and its current and future features
- Create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and prevent fraud and abuse
- Enable discovery, listings, messaging, communities, notifications, and premium services
- Verify identities, businesses, or eligibility where we offer verification features
- Send service, security, and (where permitted) marketing communications
- Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our terms
- Analyze aggregated or pseudonymized usage to improve reliability and user experience

Our legal bases may include contract performance, legitimate interests, consent (where required), and legal obligation, depending on the activity and applicable law.

## 5. Communications and preferences

We may send transactional messages (verification, security alerts, service updates) necessary to operate your account. Optional marketing or personalized recommendations are used only according to your settings and applicable law. You can adjust certain preferences in the app and unsubscribe from promotional email where offered.

- With other users, as you choose (for example public listings or messages)

Current and future payment processors, identity verification vendors, analytics tools, and moderation systems will be described in updates to this policy or related notices before material new processing begins, where required by law.
