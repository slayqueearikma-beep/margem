## 1. Overview

These Marketplace Rules govern how users interact on MarGem's discovery and connection platform. MarGem helps buyers find businesses and helps sellers reach customers in Morocco.

**MarGem is not a marketplace payment intermediary.** Transactions occur directly between buyers and sellers unless future payment features are introduced with separate terms.

---

## 2. Platform Role

MarGem provides:

- Discovery tools (search, categories, maps, featured placement)
- Business storefronts and product/service listings
- Communication tools (in-app messaging, call, WhatsApp, email)
- Reviews and ratings
- Verification indicators (discretionary, not a guarantee)
- Reporting and moderation

MarGem does **not**:

- Process buyer-to-seller payments
- Guarantee product quality, delivery, or service outcomes
- Act as agent, broker, or escrow for transactions
- Employ or endorse listed businesses (unless explicitly stated)

---

## 3. Rules for Buyers

Buyers agree to:

1. Use MarGem for lawful discovery and connection purposes
2. Submit honest reviews based on genuine experiences
3. Not submit false reports or abuse reporting tools
4. Conduct due diligence before transacting with sellers
5. Resolve purchase disputes directly with sellers
6. Not harass sellers or misuse contact information
7. Comply with [Review & Rating Policy](../marketplace/review-rating-policy.md)

---

## 4. Rules for Sellers and Businesses

Sellers agree to:

1. Maintain accurate storefront and listing information
2. Respond to inquiries in a timely and professional manner
3. Honor advertised prices and availability where possible, or update listings promptly
4. Comply with all applicable Moroccan business, tax, and licensing laws
5. Not list prohibited products or services
6. Complete verification honestly when requested
7. Comply with [Seller Terms](seller-terms.md) and [Business Listing Policy](business-listing-policy.md)

---

## 5. Rules for Professionals and Service Providers

Lawyers, accountants, consultants, and other professionals must:

- Accurately represent qualifications and licenses
- Not provide services requiring licensure without valid authorization
- Include appropriate disclaimers (see [Professional Services Disclaimer](../disclaimers/professional-services-disclaimer.md))
- Not guarantee outcomes that cannot be legally promised

---

## 6. Rules for Restaurants and Food Businesses

Food businesses must:

- Comply with Moroccan food safety and hygiene regulations
- Accurately describe menu items, allergens, and pricing
- Not misrepresent restaurant status, hours, or delivery capabilities
- Maintain current health and operating permits as required by law

---

## 7. Rules for Automotive Dealers and Services

Automotive listings must:

- Accurately describe vehicle condition, mileage, and history
- Comply with Moroccan automotive sales regulations
- Not misrepresent ownership, accident history, or mechanical condition
- See [Automotive Listings Disclaimer](../disclaimers/automotive-listings-disclaimer.md)

---

## 8. Rules for Agencies and Freelancers

Agencies and freelancers must:

- Clearly identify whether they represent a company or operate as an individual
- Accurately describe services, deliverables, and pricing models
- Not misrepresent portfolio work or client relationships

---

## 9. Listings Standards

All listings must:

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Accuracy** | Truthful titles, descriptions, and images |
| **Category** | Correct category assignment |
| **Pricing** | Displayed in MAD unless otherwise stated; negotiable prices clearly marked |
| **Images** | Original or licensed; no watermarked competitor images |
| **Availability** | Current status (active, paused, hidden) reflects reality |
| **Location** | Business operates in stated city/area |

Prohibited listings are defined in our [Prohibited Content Policy](../trust-safety/prohibited-content-policy.md).

---

## 10. Pricing and Payment

- Prices on MarGem are **informational** and **negotiable** unless stated otherwise
- Payment occurs **off-platform** between buyer and seller
- Displayed payment methods (cash, bank transfer, mobile payment) are seller preferences, not MarGem payment processing
- See [Payment Terms](payment-terms.md)

---

## 11. Disputes

- **Buyer-seller disputes:** Resolve directly between parties
- **Platform policy violations:** Report via in-app tools or safety@margem.ma
- **MarGem subscription disputes:** See [Dispute Resolution Policy](../terms/dispute-resolution.md)

---

## 12. Enforcement

Violations may result in listing removal, verification revocation, account suspension, or permanent ban. See [Trust & Safety Policy](../trust-safety/trust-safety-policy.md).
