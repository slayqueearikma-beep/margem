# MarGem Business Verification Standards

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

These standards define the criteria MarGem staff use when reviewing business verification requests. Meeting these standards does not guarantee approval; MarGem retains sole discretion.

---

## 2. Minimum Requirements

To be considered for verification, a seller must:

1. Have a **verified email address** on their MarGem account
2. Maintain a **complete storefront** (name, description, city, contact)
3. Have at least **one active listing** (product or service)
4. Have **no active policy violations** or suspensions
5. Operate a **legitimate business** in Morocco

---

## 3. Information Reviewed

MarGem staff review the following:

| Item | Standard |
|------|----------|
| Business name | Consistent with public presence; not misleading |
| Description | Accurate and relevant to listed category |
| Location | Matches stated city; coordinates reasonable |
| Contact details | Valid phone or WhatsApp number |
| Listings | Comply with [Business Listing Policy](business-listing-policy.md) |
| Account history | No fraud, spam, or abuse reports |

---

## 4. Category-Specific Standards

### Restaurants and Food
- Business name consistent with food service operations
- No prohibited food items

### Healthcare
- Clear disclaimer that MarGem does not verify medical licensure (see [Medical Information Disclaimer](../disclaimers/medical-information-disclaimer.md))
- No claims of guaranteed medical outcomes

### Automotive
- Accurate vehicle descriptions if applicable
- See [Automotive Listings Disclaimer](../disclaimers/automotive-listings-disclaimer.md)

### Professional Services
- Accurate representation of services offered
- See [Professional Services Disclaimer](../disclaimers/professional-services-disclaimer.md)

---

## 5. Rejection Reasons

Common reasons for rejection:

- Incomplete storefront information
- Mismatched business name and category
- Suspected fake or duplicate account
- Policy violations on account
- Unverifiable contact information
- Prohibited category or content

---

## 6. Reapplication

Rejected sellers may reapply after addressing the stated reason for rejection. Repeated rejections may result in verification restrictions.

---

## 7. Future Enhancements

MarGem may introduce enhanced verification tiers (e.g., document upload, license verification) with updated standards and separate terms.
