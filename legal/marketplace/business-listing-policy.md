# Dribex Business Listing Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets standards for business storefronts, product listings, and service listings on Dribex.

---

## 2. Storefront Requirements

Every seller storefront must include:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Business name | Legal or trading name; no misleading names |
| Description | Accurate summary of business activities |
| City | Must match actual operating location |
| Address | Physical address or service area description |
| Category | Primary business category |
| Contact method | At least one valid contact method (phone or WhatsApp recommended) |

Optional but recommended: logo, cover image, opening hours, website, social media links, GPS coordinates.

---

## 3. Product Listing Requirements

| Field | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| Title | Clear, descriptive, no keyword stuffing |
| Description | Accurate product details, condition, specifications |
| Price | In MAD; mark as negotiable if applicable |
| Images | Minimum one clear image; up to plan limit (higher for Seller Pro) |
| Category | Correct product category |
| Availability | Active, paused, or hidden status must reflect reality |

---

## 4. Service Listing Requirements

| Field | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| Title | Service name and scope |
| Description | Deliverables, duration, prerequisites |
| Price | Fixed, hourly, or "contact for quote" — clearly stated |
| Service area | Geographic coverage |
| Credentials | Accurate representation of licenses (where applicable) |

---

## 5. Image and Media Standards

- Use original photos or properly licensed images
- No watermarks from competing platforms (unless you own the content)
- No sexually explicit, violent, or misleading imagery
- Product images should represent the actual item or service
- Video content must comply with all content policies

---

## 6. Featured and Premium Listings

Featured placement requires an active **Seller Pro** subscription. Featured listings must still comply with all listing standards. Dribex may remove featured status for policy violations without refund.

---

## 7. Listing Moderation

Dribex may:

- Hide or remove listings that violate policies
- Reassign incorrect categories
- Request corrections before approval
- Restrict listing visibility pending verification
