# MarGem Business Verification Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

MarGem offers business verification to help users identify businesses that have submitted information for review. This policy explains the verification process, what verification means, and what it does not mean.

---

## 2. What Verification Is

Verification is a **discretionary review** by MarGem staff of a seller's submitted business information. A verified badge indicates that:

- The seller requested verification
- MarGem staff reviewed the submission
- No obvious policy violations were identified at the time of review

---

## 3. What Verification Is Not

Verification does **not** mean that MarGem:

- Guarantees the quality of products or services
- Confirms legal licensure, tax compliance, or regulatory approval
- Endorses or recommends the business
- Assumes liability for the business's conduct or transactions
- Has conducted on-site inspections or background checks (unless explicitly stated)

---

## 4. Verification Process

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1. Request | Seller submits verification request via the Platform |
| 2. Review | MarGem staff reviews business information |
| 3. Decision | Status set to `verified`, `rejected`, or remains `pending` |
| 4. Notification | Seller receives in-app notification of the decision |
| 5. Display | Verified badge shown on storefront (if approved) |

**Seller Pro subscribers** receive priority in the review queue but are not automatically verified.

---

## 5. Verification Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `unverified` | Seller has not requested verification |
| `pending` | Verification request under review |
| `verified` | Verification approved |
| `rejected` | Verification denied (seller may reapply after corrections) |

---

## 6. Revocation

MarGem may revoke verification if:

- Business information is found to be inaccurate or misleading
- The seller violates Platform policies
- Fraud or impersonation is detected
- The business ceases operations or the account is terminated

---

## 7. Appeals

Sellers may appeal verification decisions by emailing sellers@margem.ma with supporting documentation. Appeals are reviewed within 10 business days.

---

## 8. Contact

- **Verification requests:** Via in-app seller dashboard
- **Appeals:** sellers@margem.ma
