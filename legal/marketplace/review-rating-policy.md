# MarGem Review & Rating Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how reviews and ratings work on MarGem to ensure they are honest, useful, and fair.

---

## 2. Who Can Review

- Only registered **buyers** with **verified email addresses** may submit reviews
- One review per buyer per seller (unique constraint)
- Sellers **cannot** review their own business
- Guest users cannot submit reviews

---

## 3. Review Content

### 3.1 Rating Categories

Reviews include four category scores (1–5):

1. Product quality
2. Service quality
3. Communication
4. Trustworthiness

### 3.2 Written Comments

- Optional text comment, maximum **500 characters**
- Must be based on a genuine experience with the seller
- Must not contain prohibited content (hate speech, threats, private information)

---

## 4. Prohibited Review Practices

| Practice | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Fake reviews (never used the business) | Prohibited |
| Paid or incentivized reviews without disclosure | Prohibited |
| Review bombing or coordinated negative campaigns | Prohibited |
| Retaliatory reviews against competitors | Prohibited |
| Reviews containing personal attacks or hate speech | Prohibited |
| Reviews with private contact information | Prohibited |
| Seller self-reviews | Prohibited |

---

## 5. Seller Rights

- Sellers may **not** remove legitimate negative reviews
- Sellers may **report** reviews that violate this policy
- Sellers may **respond** to reviews (feature may be added in future updates)
- Sellers must not harass or retaliate against reviewers

---

## 6. Moderation

MarGem may remove reviews that:

- Violate this policy or [Prohibited Content Policy](../trust-safety/prohibited-content-policy.md)
- Are clearly fake or fraudulent
- Contain personal information or illegal content
- Are submitted from compromised or duplicate accounts

MarGem does not remove reviews solely because they are negative.

---

## 7. Reporting Reviews

Report inappropriate reviews via in-app reporting or safety@margem.ma.
