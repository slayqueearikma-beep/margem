# MarGem User Generated Content Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Definition

**User Generated Content ("UGC")** includes any content you create, upload, or submit on MarGem, including:

- Business storefronts and descriptions
- Product and service listings (text, images, video)
- Reviews and ratings
- In-app messages
- Reports and feedback
- Profile information

---

## 2. Your Responsibilities

You are solely responsible for your UGC. By submitting UGC, you represent that:

1. You own or have the necessary rights to the content
2. The content is accurate and not misleading
3. The content does not violate any law or third-party rights
4. The content complies with all MarGem policies

---

## 3. License Grant

By submitting UGC, you grant MarGem a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable license to use, host, store, reproduce, modify (for formatting), display, and distribute your UGC solely to operate, promote, and improve the Platform.

This license ends when your UGC is deleted from our systems, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business purposes (e.g., backups, dispute resolution).

---

## 4. Content Standards

UGC must not contain:

- Illegal content or content promoting illegal activity
- Hate speech, harassment, or threats
- Sexually explicit content, especially involving minors
- Personal information of others without consent
- Spam, malware, or phishing content
- Counterfeit or infringing material
- Content that violates [Prohibited Content Policy](../trust-safety/prohibited-content-policy.md)

---

## 5. Moderation

MarGem may review, remove, or restrict UGC that violates our policies. We use a combination of:

- User reports
- Automated signals (where implemented)
- Human review by trust & safety staff

See [Content Moderation Policy](../trust-safety/content-moderation-policy.md).

---

## 6. Copyright

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without permission, see our [DMCA-Style Takedown Policy](../trust-safety/dmca-takedown-policy.md).
