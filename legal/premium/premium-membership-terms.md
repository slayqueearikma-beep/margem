# Dribex Seller Pro Membership Terms

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Plan Overview

**Seller Pro** (plan code: `seller_pro`) is Dribex's premium subscription for sellers and businesses.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Price** | 199 MAD per 30-day period |
| **Billing** | Recurring monthly (30-day cycles) |
| **Eligibility** | Registered sellers with verified email |

---

## 2. Benefits

Seller Pro includes:

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Featured placement** | Enhanced visibility in search results and category browsing |
| **Premium badge** | Visual indicator on your storefront |
| **Advanced analytics** | Profile views, inquiries, favorites, contact clicks, response time metrics |
| **Extra media uploads** | Higher limits for product/service images and video |
| **Verification priority** | Faster review of verification requests (not automatic approval) |

---

## 3. Limitations

Seller Pro does **not**:

- Guarantee a specific number of views, inquiries, or sales
- Automatically verify your business
- Remove the requirement to comply with all Platform policies
- Provide legal, tax, or business advice
- Include in-platform payment processing for buyer transactions
