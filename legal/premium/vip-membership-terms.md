# MarGem VIP Membership Terms (MarGem Plus)

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Plan Overview

**MarGem Plus** (plan code: `buyer_premium`, displayed as VIP in admin interfaces) is MarGem's premium subscription for buyers.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Price** | 49 MAD per 30-day period |
| **Billing** | Recurring monthly (30-day cycles) |
| **Eligibility** | Registered buyers with verified email |

---

## 2. Benefits

MarGem Plus includes:

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Saved search sync** | Saved searches synchronized across devices |
| **Personalized recommendations** | Enhanced discovery based on browsing history and preferences |
| **Priority support** | Faster response from support team |
| **Early access** | Early access to featured listings and new features |

---

## 3. Limitations

MarGem Plus does **not**:

- Guarantee specific recommendations or discovery outcomes
- Provide discounts on products or services listed by sellers
- Include buyer-seller transaction protections (transactions occur off-platform)
- Remove the requirement to comply with Platform policies

---

## 4. Personalization

"Personalized recommendations" are based on your activity on MarGem (browsing, favorites, saved searches). MarGem does not currently use artificial intelligence or machine learning for recommendations. See [AI Usage Disclosure](../privacy/ai-usage-disclosure.md).
