## 3. Free Standard Accounts

Buyer and seller accounts are **free by default**. Paid plans are optional upgrades that unlock additional platform features. Creating a business storefront does not require a paid subscription unless you choose Seller Pro.

## 4. Billing and Payment

### 4.1 Currency

All prices are in **Moroccan Dirham (MAD)**.

### 4.2 Payment Processing

When live billing is enabled, payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. Dribex does **not** store full payment card numbers.

### 4.3 Current Billing Status

Self-serve subscription billing may not be available in all environments. During pre-launch or when billing is unavailable, contact **billing@dribex.ma** or **support@dribex.ma** for manual activation.

### 4.4 Taxes

Prices may exclude applicable taxes. You are responsible for any taxes on your subscription.

## 5. Renewals

- Subscriptions automatically renew every **30 days** unless cancelled before the renewal date
- You will be charged the then-current price at renewal
- We will notify you of price changes with reasonable advance notice
- Failed renewal payments may result in downgrade to the free standard tier

---

## 6. Cancellation

- Cancel anytime through account settings or by emailing billing@dribex.ma
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- No partial refunds for unused days unless required by law (see [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md))
- Premium features remain active until the end of the paid period
