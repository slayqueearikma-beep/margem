## 1. Introduction

MarGem ("**MarGem**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") operates a local discovery and marketplace platform available on Android, iOS, and Web. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use MarGem services (the "**Platform**").

By using MarGem, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before processing your personal information.

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Personal Information** | Information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with an individual. |
| **Processing** | Any operation performed on personal information, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion. |
| **User** | Any person who accesses or uses the Platform, including guests, buyers, sellers, and staff. |
| **Seller / Business** | A User who maintains a storefront, listing, or professional profile on MarGem. |
| **Guest** | A User who browses the Platform without creating an account. |
| **Device Data** | Technical information generated by your device or browser when you access the Platform. |

This Privacy Policy applies to:

## 5. Information We Collect

### 5.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information**
- Email address, display name, password (stored as a cryptographic hash), account type (buyer, seller, or both), phone number (optional)
- Email verification status and timestamps

**Seller and Business Information**
- Business name, description, address, city, and geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude)
- Contact details: phone, WhatsApp, website, and social media links (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- Business images (logo, cover photo), product and service listings, pricing in Moroccan Dirham (MAD)
- Opening hours, service areas, delivery options, and accepted payment methods (display only)
- Verification status and related communications

**User-Generated Content**
- Reviews and ratings (category scores and comments)
- In-app messages and inquiries (buyer–seller conversations)
- **City community chat** messages, reactions, mentions, and attachments in Moroccan city channels (membership required)
- Reports submitted about listings, businesses, users, or community messages
- Favorites, follows, and saved searches

**Communications**
- Support requests and correspondence with our team
- Feedback and survey responses

### 5.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage and Interaction Data**
- Search queries, category browsing, listing views, and contact interactions (call, WhatsApp, email, SMS, website clicks)
- Favorites, follows, recently viewed items, and saved searches
- In-app notification interactions

**Device and Technical Data**
- Device type, operating system, app version, language preference
- IP address, request identifiers, session metadata
- Refresh token metadata: device name, IP address, user agent, last seen timestamp
- Crash reports and error diagnostics (when crash reporting is enabled)

**Location Data**
- Approximate or precise device location, only when you grant permission, to display nearby businesses on the map and improve local discovery
- Business location coordinates provided by sellers for storefront display

### 5.3 Guest Users

Guests may browse, search, and save favorites locally on their device without creating an account. Guest favorites are stored on the device using local storage and may be migrated to your account if you register later.

### 5.4 Information from Third Parties

- **Firebase Authentication** (optional): If you sign in using a supported identity provider, we may receive a provider user identifier
- **Payment processors** (when subscription billing is enabled): Transaction identifiers and subscription status — we do not store full payment card numbers

We do not purchase personal information from data brokers.

## 13. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 principles, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- Encrypted local storage of authentication tokens
- Access controls and role-based permissions for staff
- Audit logging for administrative actions
- Rate limiting and abuse detection

See our [Security Policy](../security/security-policy.md) for more information.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact security@margem.ma immediately.
