# MarGem Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how MarGem uses cookies and similar technologies on our **Web platform** (margem.ma, margem.app). MarGem **mobile applications** use local device storage rather than browser cookies.

---

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

---

## 3. Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Session token | Maintain logged-in state on Web | Session |
| CSRF protection | Prevent cross-site request forgery | Session |
| Language preference | Remember selected language | 1 year |

These cookies are essential for Platform operation and cannot be disabled.

### 3.2 Functional

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Cookie consent | Remember your cookie preferences | 1 year |
| UI preferences | Theme, layout preferences | 1 year |

### 3.3 Analytics (if enabled)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Analytics ID | Measure Web usage and improve services | 1 year |

Analytics cookies are only set with your consent where required by law.

### 3.4 Third-Party

| Provider | Purpose | Policy |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Google Maps | Map display (when enabled) | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

---

## 4. Mobile App Storage

MarGem mobile apps use:

| Storage | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Secure storage** | Encrypted authentication tokens |
| **Shared preferences** | Language, onboarding state, guest favorites |
| **Local cache** | App performance and offline data |

These are not browser cookies but serve similar functions. Manage by clearing app data or deleting your account.

---

## 5. Your Choices

### Web
- Use our cookie consent banner to accept or reject non-essential cookies
- Configure your browser to block or delete cookies
- Note: blocking essential cookies may affect Platform functionality

### Mobile
- Manage permissions (location, notifications) in device settings
- Clear app data to remove local storage

---

## 6. Do Not Track

MarGem does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals. We honor cookie consent preferences where required by law.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via the Platform.
