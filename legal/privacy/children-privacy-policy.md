# MarGem Children's Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

MarGem is not directed to children. This policy explains our practices regarding children's personal information.

---

## 2. Age Requirement

MarGem requires users to be at least **16 years old** (or the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction, whichever is higher) to create an account.

---

## 3. No Knowing Collection

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 16, we will:

1. Delete the account and associated data promptly
2. Notify the parent or guardian if contact information is available
3. Take steps to prevent re-registration

---

## 4. Parental Rights

If you believe your child under 16 has created a MarGem account, contact us immediately:

- **Email:** privacy@margem.app
- **Subject:** "Child Account Report"

We will investigate and delete the account within 5 business days.

---

## 5. Guest Browsing

Unauthenticated guest browsing does not require personal information. Guest favorites are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to MarGem servers.

---

## 6. COPPA and International Compliance

While MarGem primarily operates in Morocco, we align with children's privacy principles under:

- **COPPA** (U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — no knowing collection from children under 13
- **GDPR** (EU) — parental consent required for children under 16 (or lower age set by member state)
- **Morocco Law 09-08** — protection of minors' personal data
