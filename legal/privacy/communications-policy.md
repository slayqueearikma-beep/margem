# MarGem Communications Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Scope

This policy governs how MarGem communicates with users and how users communicate with each other on the Platform.

---

## 2. MarGem-to-User Communications

### 2.1 Transactional Communications

MarGem sends communications necessary for Platform operation:

| Type | Channel | Consent Required |
|------|---------|------------------|
| Email verification | Email | No (service necessity) |
| Password reset | Email | No (service necessity) |
| Account security alerts | Email, in-app | No (legitimate interest) |
| Verification decisions | In-app notification | No (service necessity) |
| Premium activation | In-app notification | No (service necessity) |
| Policy updates | Email, in-app | No (legal obligation) |

### 2.2 Platform Announcements

MarGem may send announcements about new features, maintenance, or platform updates via in-app notifications and email. Admin announcements may target: all users, buyers, sellers, or premium subscribers.

### 2.3 Marketing Communications

Marketing emails require your consent where required by law. You may opt out at any time via the unsubscribe link or account settings.

---

## 3. User-to-User Communications

### 3.1 In-App Messaging

Registered users with verified email may send in-app messages. Messages must comply with [Community Guidelines](../terms/community-guidelines.md) and [Acceptable Use Policy](../terms/acceptable-use-policy.md).

### 3.2 External Contact

Users may contact each other via call, WhatsApp, email, SMS, or website links displayed on listings. These communications occur outside MarGem and are not monitored by MarGem unless reported.

---

## 4. Prohibited Communications

- Spam, unsolicited bulk messages, or chain letters
- Harassment, threats, or hate speech
- Phishing or fraudulent messages
- Messages promoting prohibited content or services
- Automated messaging or bots

---

## 5. Monitoring

MarGem does not routinely monitor in-app messages. Messages may be reviewed when reported for policy violations or as required by law.
