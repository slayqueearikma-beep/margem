## 1. Our Commitment

Dribex is committed to maintaining a safe, trustworthy platform for discovering local businesses in Morocco. We invest in policies, tools, and staff to detect and address abuse.

---

## 2. Reporting Abuse

### 2.1 In-App Reporting

Use the **Report** button on any listing, business profile, review, or message to submit a report with a reason and optional details.

### 2.2 Email Reporting

For urgent or complex matters, contact safety@dribex.ma with:

- URL or name of the reported content/account
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots or evidence (if available)
- Your contact information

### 2.3 Anonymous Reports

Reports may be submitted by authenticated or anonymous users. We encourage account holders to report for follow-up communication.

## 3. Threat Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Fake listings** | Non-existent businesses, stock photos misrepresented as owned, duplicate scam accounts |
| **Fraud** | Advance-fee scams, fake payment requests, phishing |
| **Scams** | Bait-and-switch pricing, non-delivery after payment, impersonation |
| **Spam** | Unsolicited bulk messages, irrelevant promotions, link farming |
| **Impersonation** | Pretending to be another business, brand, or Dribex staff |
| **Illegal products** | Counterfeit goods, controlled substances, stolen items |
| **Dangerous services** | Unlicensed medical procedures, unsafe automotive modifications |
| **Harassment** | Threats, stalking, hate speech, doxxing |
| **Child safety** | Content involving minors in any exploitative context (zero tolerance) |

---

## 4. Investigation Process

1. **Triage:** Reports are categorized by severity and urgency
2. **Review:** Trust & safety staff review reported content and account history
3. **Action:** Appropriate enforcement action is taken
4. **Notification:** Affected users are notified where appropriate
5. **Documentation:** Actions are logged in admin audit records

**Target response times:**

| Severity | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Critical (child safety, imminent harm) | < 24 hours |
| High (fraud, impersonation) | 24–72 hours |
| Standard | 3–5 business days |

---

## 5. Enforcement Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Warning** | Formal notice of policy violation |
| **Content removal** | Listing, review, or message removed |
| **Visibility restriction** | Reduced search ranking or hidden from discovery |
| **Feature restriction** | Messaging, listing creation, or verification disabled |
| **Temporary suspension** | Account access suspended for a defined period |
| **Permanent ban** | Account permanently terminated |
| **Verification revocation** | Verified badge removed |
| **Law enforcement referral** | Reported to authorities for illegal activity |

---

## 6. Appeals Process

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error:

1. Email safety@dribex.ma within **30 days** of the action
2. Include your account email, the action taken, and your explanation
3. Provide supporting evidence if available
4. Appeals are reviewed by a different staff member within **10 business days**
5. Decisions on appeal are final, except where required by law

---

## 7. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Dribex cooperates with law enforcement agencies in Morocco and other jurisdictions when presented with valid legal process. Emergency requests involving imminent harm may be escalated through security@dribex.ma.
