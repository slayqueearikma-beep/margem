# MarGem Content Moderation Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how MarGem moderates user-generated content to maintain platform quality and safety.

---

## 2. Moderation Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **User reports** | Community-flagged content reviewed by staff |
| **Proactive review** | Staff review of verification requests, featured listings, and flagged accounts |
| **Automated signals** | Rate limiting, duplicate detection, and abuse pattern recognition |
| **Admin actions** | Staff-initiated moderation via admin console |

MarGem does not currently use AI-based content moderation. Human review is the primary method.

---

## 3. Moderation Scope

We moderate:

- Business storefronts and listings
- Product and service descriptions and images
- Reviews and ratings
- In-app messages (when reported)
- User profiles and display names
- Reports submitted by users

---

## 4. Moderation Actions

| Action | When Used |
|--------|-----------|
| No action | Content complies with policies |
| Request edit | Minor issues correctable by user |
| Hide content | Pending review or policy concern |
| Remove content | Clear policy violation |
| Restrict account | Repeated or serious violations |
| Escalate | Potential illegal activity |

---

## 5. Transparency

- Users are notified of significant moderation actions affecting their account
- General moderation statistics may be published in transparency reports (future)
- We do not disclose specific staff reviewer identities
