# MarGem DMCA-Style Takedown Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

MarGem respects intellectual property rights. This policy provides a process for rights holders to report copyright and trademark infringement on the Platform, modeled on the notice-and-takedown framework used under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and adapted for Moroccan law.

---

## 2. Filing a Takedown Notice

To submit a takedown notice, email **copyright@margem.ma** with:

### Required Information

1. **Your identity:** Full legal name, organization (if applicable), and contact information (email, phone, address)
2. **Identification of the work:** Description of the copyrighted work or trademark you claim has been infringed
3. **Identification of the infringing material:** URL, listing ID, business name, or sufficient description to locate the content on MarGem
4. **Good faith statement:** A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or the law
5. **Accuracy statement:** A statement, under penalty of perjury (where applicable), that the information in the notice is accurate and you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder
6. **Signature:** Physical or electronic signature of the rights holder or authorized agent

---

## 3. Our Response

Upon receiving a valid notice:

1. We will acknowledge receipt within **5 business days**
2. We will review the notice and remove or disable access to the reported content if the claim appears valid
3. We will notify the user who posted the content
4. We will document the action in our records

---

## 4. Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification to copyright@margem.ma including:

1. Your name and contact information
2. Identification of the removed content and its location before removal
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification
4. Consent to jurisdiction of courts in Morocco (or your jurisdiction as applicable)
5. Your physical or electronic signature

We may restore the content within 10–14 business days unless the original complainant initiates legal proceedings.

---

## 5. Repeat Infringers

Accounts with multiple valid infringement notices may be permanently terminated.

---

## 6. Misrepresentation

Knowingly submitting false takedown notices or counter-notifications may result in liability under applicable law.
