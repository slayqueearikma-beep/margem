# MarGem Prohibited Content Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

The following content is **prohibited** on MarGem. Violations result in immediate content removal and may lead to account termination.

---

## 2. Prohibited Categories

### 2.1 Illegal Content
- Content that violates Moroccan law or applicable international law
- Stolen goods, counterfeit products, and pirated media
- Controlled substances and drug paraphernalia (except as permitted by law)
- Weapons and explosives (except licensed dealers with proper authorization)
- Human trafficking, exploitation, or illegal services

### 2.2 Child Safety (Zero Tolerance)
- Sexual content involving minors
- Content that sexualizes, grooms, or endangers children
- Accounts operated by or targeting children under 16

### 2.3 Fraud and Deception
- Fake businesses, listings, or reviews
- Phishing links, malware, and scam schemes
- Misleading pricing, bait-and-switch tactics
- Impersonation of individuals, brands, or MarGem

### 2.4 Harmful Content
- Hate speech targeting protected characteristics
- Harassment, threats, and intimidation
- Content promoting violence, terrorism, or self-harm
- Graphic violence or gore

### 2.5 Sexual Content
- Pornography and sexually explicit services
- Escort services and adult entertainment (unless permitted by local law with age restrictions)

### 2.6 Regulated Services (Without Proper Authorization)
- Unlicensed medical treatment or prescription sales
- Unlicensed legal, financial, or investment advice presented as licensed
- Unregistered pharmaceutical sales
- Pyramid schemes and multi-level marketing scams

### 2.7 Dangerous Products and Services
- Recalled products
- Unsafe automotive modifications
- Unverified health supplements with medical claims
- Services that pose imminent physical danger

### 2.8 Spam and Platform Abuse
- Unsolicited bulk messaging
- Keyword stuffing and search manipulation
- Automated account creation and content posting
- Scraping and unauthorized data collection

### 2.9 Intellectual Property Violations
- Copyright-infringing images, text, or media
- Trademark misuse and brand impersonation
