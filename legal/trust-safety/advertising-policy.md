# MarGem Advertising Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

MarGem does **not** operate third-party advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Facebook Audience Network). This policy governs **internal promotional placement** and future advertising features.

---

## 2. Current Promotional Features

MarGem offers **paid visibility** through subscriptions, not third-party ads:

| Feature | Description | Eligibility |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Featured listings** | Enhanced placement in discovery results | Seller Pro subscribers |
| **Premium badge** | Visual trust indicator on storefront | Seller Pro subscribers |
| **Premium-first ranking** | Priority in search and category results | Seller Pro subscribers |
| **Featured businesses** | Highlighted on home and discovery screens | Seller Pro subscribers |

These are **platform membership benefits**, not advertisements sold to third parties.

---

## 3. Platform Announcements

MarGem may send platform announcements to users via:

- In-app notifications
- Email (with consent where required)

Announcements are MarGem communications, not paid third-party advertisements. Admin announcements may target audiences: all users, buyers, sellers, or premium subscribers.

---

## 4. Seller Promotional Content

Sellers may promote their own businesses through their listings and storefronts, subject to:

- [Business Listing Policy](../marketplace/business-listing-policy.md)
- [Prohibited Content Policy](prohibited-content-policy.md)
- No misleading claims or false urgency tactics
- No promotion of prohibited products or services

---

## 5. Future Advertising

If MarGem introduces third-party advertising in the future:

- This policy will be updated with specific terms
- Users will be notified of material changes
- Cookie and privacy policies will be updated accordingly
- Users may be offered opt-out mechanisms where required by law

---

## 6. Prohibited Advertising Practices

Even for self-promotion, the following are prohibited:

- False or misleading claims
- Bait-and-switch pricing
- Promotion of illegal products or services
- Spam or unsolicited bulk messaging
- Impersonation of competitors or MarGem

---
