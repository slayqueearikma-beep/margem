# MarGem Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## Our Community Promise

MarGem connects people with local businesses across Morocco. These Community Guidelines help keep our platform trustworthy, respectful, and useful for everyone.

---

## 1. Be Honest

- Provide accurate business information, pricing, and availability
- Write reviews based on real experiences — not incentives, retaliation, or fake accounts
- Do not misrepresent your identity, credentials, or affiliation

---

## 2. Be Respectful

- Treat other users, businesses, and MarGem staff with respect
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, or discriminatory content
- Disagree constructively; do not attack individuals

---

## 3. Be Safe

- Do not list or promote dangerous, illegal, or prohibited products and services
- Report suspicious listings, scams, and impersonation immediately
- Meet in safe locations for in-person transactions; MarGem does not supervise offline interactions

---

## 4. Be Helpful

- Use clear photos and descriptions in listings
- Respond to inquiries promptly and professionally
- Leave constructive reviews that help other users make informed decisions

---

## 5. Protect Privacy

- Do not share others' personal information without consent
- Use in-app messaging for initial contact when possible
- Respect boundaries — no unsolicited spam or harassment

---

## 6. Respect Intellectual Property

- Only upload content you own or have permission to use
- Do not copy competitor listings, photos, or descriptions
- Report copyright violations to copyright@margem.ma

---

## 7. Use MarGem as Intended

MarGem is for **local discovery and connection** — not for:

- Unrelated advertising or link farming
- Political campaigning unrelated to business listings
- Off-topic spam or chain messages
- Circumventing moderation or verification

---

## 8. Consequences

Violations may result in:

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor / first offense | Warning and content removal |
| Repeated violations | Feature restrictions or temporary suspension |
| Serious violations | Permanent account termination |
| Illegal activity | Account termination and referral to authorities |

You may appeal moderation decisions as described in our [Content Moderation Policy](../trust-safety/content-moderation-policy.md).

---

## 9. Contact

- **Report abuse:** In-app Report button or safety@margem.ma
- **Questions:** support@margem.ma
