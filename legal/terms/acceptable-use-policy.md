# MarGem Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") defines permitted and prohibited uses of the MarGem platform. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent termination.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

You may use MarGem to:

- Discover local businesses, products, and services in Morocco
- Create and manage legitimate business listings and storefronts
- Communicate with other users through provided tools
- Submit honest reviews based on genuine experiences
- Report policy violations or safety concerns
- Subscribe to premium features in accordance with subscription terms

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use MarGem to:

### 3.1 Illegal Activity
- Violate any applicable law, regulation, or court order
- Facilitate illegal goods, services, or transactions
- Engage in money laundering, terrorism financing, or sanctions evasion

### 3.2 Fraud and Deception
- Create fake accounts, listings, or reviews
- Impersonate individuals, businesses, or MarGem staff
- Mislead users about pricing, availability, location, or credentials
- Manipulate ratings, rankings, or verification status

### 3.3 Harmful Content
- Post content that is defamatory, harassing, threatening, or hateful
- Share sexually explicit content involving minors (zero tolerance)
- Promote violence, self-harm, or dangerous activities
- Distribute malware, phishing links, or spam

### 3.4 Platform Abuse
- Scrape, crawl, or harvest data without written authorization
- Circumvent rate limits, access controls, or security measures
- Use bots or automated tools to create accounts or content
- Interfere with Platform operation or other users' access

### 3.5 Commercial Abuse
- List counterfeit, stolen, or recalled products
- Offer unlicensed professional, medical, or financial services
- Engage in pyramid schemes, unsolicited bulk messaging, or referral fraud
- Use MarGem solely to redirect traffic to off-platform scams

### 3.6 Privacy Violations
- Collect personal information from users without consent
- Publish others' private information (doxxing)
- Use MarGem data for unauthorized marketing or profiling

---

## 4. Enforcement

Violations are handled under our [Content Moderation Policy](../trust-safety/content-moderation-policy.md) and [Trust & Safety Policy](../trust-safety/trust-safety-policy.md). Enforcement actions include warnings, content removal, feature restrictions, suspension, and permanent bans.

---

Report violations via in-app reporting or email safety@margem.ma.
