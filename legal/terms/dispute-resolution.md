## 1. Scope

This policy governs dispute resolution for matters arising from your use of the MarGem Platform.

---

## 2. Informal Resolution

Before initiating formal proceedings, you agree to contact MarGem and attempt to resolve the dispute informally:

1. Email **legal@margem.ma** with a description of the dispute
2. MarGem will respond within **15 business days**
3. Both parties will negotiate in good faith for **30 days**

Most disputes can be resolved through informal communication.

---

## 3. User-to-User Disputes

Disputes between buyers and sellers (e.g., product quality, payment, delivery) must be resolved **directly between the parties**. MarGem:

- Is not a party to buyer-seller transactions
- Does not mediate commercial disputes (but may assist with policy violations)
- Encourages users to document transactions and communicate clearly

Report fraud or policy violations to safety@margem.ma.

---

## 4. Disputes with MarGem

If informal resolution fails, disputes between you and MarGem shall be resolved as described below.

---

## 5. Arbitration

> **Note for counsel:** The arbitration clause below should be reviewed for enforceability under Moroccan law and applicable consumer protection regulations before publication.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, disputes between you and MarGem that cannot be resolved informally shall be resolved by **binding arbitration** administered under the rules of the **Moroccan Center for Mediation and Arbitration (CMAC)** or, by mutual agreement, another recognized arbitration body.

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Seat** | Casablanca, Morocco |
| **Language** | English, French, or Arabic (as agreed) |
| **Arbitrator** | Single arbitrator mutually agreed or appointed per CMAC rules |
| **Costs** | Each party bears its own costs; arbitrator fees shared equally unless awarded otherwise |

---

## 6. Class Action Waiver

To the extent permitted by law, you agree to resolve disputes with MarGem on an **individual basis** and waive any right to participate in a class action, collective action, or representative proceeding.

---

## 7. Exceptions

The following may be brought in court:

- Intellectual property infringement claims
- Injunctive or equitable relief
- Small claims court matters within jurisdictional limits
- Matters where arbitration is prohibited by applicable consumer protection law

---

## 8. Governing Courts

Where arbitration does not apply, disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **Casablanca, Morocco**. See [Governing Law](governing-law.md).
