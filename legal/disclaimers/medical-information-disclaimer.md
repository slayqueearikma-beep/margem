## 1. Not Medical Advice

MarGem is a **discovery platform**. Information about healthcare providers, clinics, pharmacies, and medical services on MarGem is provided by third-party businesses for **informational purposes only**. **Nothing on MarGem constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

---

## 2. No Verification of Medical Credentials

MarGem does **not**:

- Verify medical licenses, board certifications, or hospital affiliations
- Confirm that healthcare providers are registered with Moroccan medical authorities
- Endorse any healthcare provider, treatment, medication, or medical device
- Guarantee the safety, efficacy, or appropriateness of any medical service

---

## 3. Emergency Situations

**MarGem is not an emergency service.** If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately:

- **Morocco emergency:** 15 (SAMU) or 141 (Police) or 150 (Fire)
- Do not use MarGem for urgent medical needs

---

## 4. User Responsibility

Before using any healthcare service found on MarGem:

- Verify the provider's medical license and credentials independently
- Consult with a qualified healthcare professional about your health needs
- Do not rely on listings, reviews, or MarGem verification as a substitute for medical judgment
- Confirm that treatments, medications, and procedures are appropriate for your condition

---

## 5. Prohibited Medical Content

The following are prohibited on MarGem:

- Sale of prescription medications without proper authorization
- Unlicensed medical practice or treatment
- False or misleading health claims
- Promotion of unapproved treatments or dangerous medical practices

Report violations to safety@margem.ma.
